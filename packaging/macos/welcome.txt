Multi-Stream Stats {VERSION}

This installs the Multi-Stream Statistics dock for OBS Studio 33 or newer.

The plugin is installed for your user account only, into:
  ~/Library/Application Support/obs-studio/plugins/obs-multistream-stats.plugin

That is the folder OBS Studio loads plugins from. Your OBS settings, scenes and profiles are not changed. An older version of the plugin in the same place is replaced; the dock's own settings are kept.

Please quit OBS Studio before you continue.
